# Privacy policy

*Effective 24 November 2026.*

Perch is an Android app that shows what is playing on your tablet and a clock. It is built so that nothing
you do in it leaves your device.

## What Perch handles, and where it goes

| Data | Why | Stored? | Sent anywhere? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| What is playing in other apps: title, artist, album, cover picture, position, play state (read from the device's media sessions) | To draw the now-playing screen and to send play, pause, next, previous and seek back to the player | Only in memory while the song plays; never written to storage | **No** |
| The device name and battery level | To label the screen and show the battery on the clock | No | **No** |
| Your three settings (how long the clock stays on, the pause delay, and the switch for opening automatically) | To remember your choices | On the device, in Perch's private preferences | **No** |

Perch does **not** read, store or transmit the content of your notifications. It holds *notification access* only
because Android ties access to other apps' media sessions to that permission.

## What Perch does not do

- It has no internet permission and makes no network requests.
- It contains no analytics, advertising, crash reporting or other third-party tracking code.
- It has no accounts, and collects no name, email address, location, contacts, files, photos or identifiers.
- Android backups of Perch's data are switched off, so your settings are not copied to the cloud or to a new device.
- It is not directed at children.

## Other apps

Perch shows information from apps such as Spotify that you run on the same device. What those apps collect is
governed by their own privacy policies, not this one. Perch is not affiliated with or endorsed by Spotify AB.
